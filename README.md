## ☕ Coffee Sales Analysis Project
# 📘 Overview
The Coffee Sales Analysis Project aims to uncover valuable insights from sales data to understand performance trends, customer preferences, and regional sales distribution.
By applying data analytics and visualization techniques, this project helps identify key business drivers that impact coffee sales performance over time.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across regions, coffee types, and sizes.
- Identify top-selling coffee products and most profitable segments.
- Detect seasonal trends and sales growth patterns.
- Provide data-driven insights to improve marketing and sales strategies.

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing
- Steps performed before analysis:
- Handled missing values and inconsistent data entries.
- Converted date columns to proper datetime format.
- Removed duplicates and outliers.
- Created new calculated columns for total sales and profit.

## 🧠 Analysis Performed
- Sales by Region: To identify areas with highest sales volume.
- Sales by Product Type & Size: To evaluate customer preferences.
- Monthly & Yearly Trends: To study seasonality in sales.
- Customer Segmentation: To analyze key customer categories driving revenue.

## 📊 Visualizations
- Visual insights were generated using Matplotlib and Seaborn.

# Example Plots:
🔹 Bar Plot — Top 10 coffee types by total sales.
🔹 Line Chart — Monthly sales trend across regions.
🔹 Count Plot — Distribution of coffee sizes purchased.
🔹 Pie Chart — Regional sales contribution.
These visuals help easily interpret how coffee sales behave across time, geography, and product categories.
